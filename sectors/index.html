US sectors

The S&P 500 split into its eleven GICS sectors, measured with the Select Sector SPDR ETFs (total return, dividends reinvested) against the SPDR S&P 500 ETF. Most sector funds start in December 1998; Real Estate begins in 2015 and Communication Services in 2018.

Leadership by year

Each column ranks the sectors from best to worst in that calendar year. The S&P 500 is outlined. Hover a cell, or choose a sector, to follow it through time.

Returns over 3, 5 and 10 years are annualized. Beta and correlation use monthly returns against the S&P 500 over the last 10 years (or since inception if shorter). Click a column to sort.
