Valuation

Is a stock cheap or expensive? Trailing P/E and EV/EBITDA for every S&P 500 company, month by month, set against the company's own last ten years and against the other companies in its sector. The two comparisons add up to a signal: undervalued, fairly valued or overvalued.

Not investment advice. "Undervalued" and "overvalued" describe where a company's multiples sit against its own past and its sector, computed automatically from public data. They are not recommendations to buy or sell, they ignore growth and risk, and they do not replace advice from a licensed financial adviser.
